the TTL follow-up review.

## Tab-create recovery contract

The exploratory `open-new-tab-recovery-deadend.md` contains proposals that this
implementation deliberately does not adopt: neither `not_found`, a matching
URL, an unchanged count, nor absence of a matching tab establishes that no tab
was created. The final contract is in
`.trellis/spec/backend/error-handling.md`.

- Pre-dispatch `may_have_created: false, retry_safe: true`: resolve the cause,
  then call without `operation_id` to begin a fresh operation.
- First recovery probe `not_found`: keep `may_have_created: true`,
  `retry_safe: false` and the original `owner_id`; set
  `reconciliation.resume_required: false` and direct `list_tabs()` inspection.
- `list_tabs()` has no `client_id` parameter. Inspect the returned identities.
- Cleanup still requires an exact client/tab/generation already registered as
  owned by this MCP task. An owner ID by itself grants no ownership.
- Unknown/unreadable probes and pending-to-missing transitions retain their
  conservative read-only recovery behavior. No `new_operation_safe: true` claim
  is added.
